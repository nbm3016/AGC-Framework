# Constitution

These six statements are the lock. They do not add parameters. `continuous_knobs = 0`.

1. The ambient theory is 14-dimensional. The fibration is \(Y^{14} \to M^4\). \(\Sigma^5\) and \(T^{1,1}\) are compact factors. They are not the ambient theory. \(\star\Psi = \Psi\) is a filter on modes. It does not delete topics before they are tested.

2. The observer stick is not physics. A conversion that uses that stick is withdrawn after comparison. Locks are dimensionless. No compact radius \(R_c\) and no GeV stick enter the lock.

3. \(N_{\mathrm{gen}}=3\) is the Module A wall-index rule on the self-dual line. It is not \(\ker D_5\).

4. \(\mathrm{Hol}(g_C)=\mathrm{SU}(3)\) is holonomy of the cone on the factor \(T^{1,1}\). It is not color and not \(g_{\mu\nu}\).

5. The canonical spectral triple of the locked 5-metric reconstructs \(T^{1,1}\). It does not reconstruct \(M^4\).

6. `fibration_class` is not uniquely determined. \(M^{p,q}\) is not selected. The second Chern number \(c_2\) and the warp slope \(k\) stay external. \(8\pi G_{\mathrm{eff}}=9/16\) is a pure number, not \(G_N\). \(T_{\mathrm{wall}}=13/2\) is not brane tension.
