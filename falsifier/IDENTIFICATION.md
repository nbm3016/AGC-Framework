# Identification

IDENTIFICATION STAMP - THREE SPINS NOT THREE COPIES - ZERO KNOBS

The locked wall-index zeros on \(j_2=r=0\) are

\[
(j_1,j_2,r) = (1/2,0,0),\ (1,0,0),\ (3/2,0,0).
\]

The first \(\mathrm{SU}(2)\) has dimension \(2j_1+1\). Those dimensions are 2, 3, and 4. The zeros are one doublet, one triplet, and one quartet.

They are three different harmonics. They are not three copies of one 4D Weyl spinor.

\(N_{\mathrm{gen}}=3\) remains the count of zeros. “Three generations” is a name, not an A-lock. Unique color is not promoted.
